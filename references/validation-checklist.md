# Validation checklist

## Structure

- [ ] The Intake Gate passed before a complete document was drafted.
- [ ] If the input was Problem-only, the response stopped at the audit and questions stage.
- [ ] Context describes the current state without proposing a solution.
- [ ] Problem names a pain or gap and includes impact.
- [ ] Problem type is identifiable: User pain, Business impact, Opportunity gap, or Competitive gap.
- [ ] Goals and Non-Goals define the scope boundary.
- [ ] Relevant optional sections were checked.
- [ ] Open Questions are separate from Future Work.
- [ ] Post-Launch checks are present when the initiative is intended to ship.

## Evidence integrity

- [ ] Every strong claim has a source, supplied Evidence, or a visible validation status.
- [ ] No metric, baseline, target, source, or user behavior was invented.
- [ ] Metric denominators, populations, and time windows are preserved when supplied.
- [ ] Evidence is not confused with interpretation or design rationale.
- [ ] Missing information is labeled `Not provided`, `Not available`, `Not applicable`, or `Needs validation`.
- [ ] Each missing-information status was supplied or confirmed by the author.
- [ ] No Metric was defined or populated from the Problem alone.

## Benchmarks and Figma

- [ ] The benchmark scope and reason for selecting examples are documented.
- [ ] Original Figma and source links are preserved.
- [ ] The exact Figma pages, sections, or frames reviewed are named when available.
- [ ] Visible Notes, annotations, comments, and comparison tables are captured when the connected tool exposes them.
- [ ] Direct observations are separated from interpretation.
- [ ] Benchmark findings are connected to a requirement, option, decision, or Open Question.
- [ ] Inaccessible Figma content is marked `Not accessible` or `Needs validation`.

## Links and artifacts

- [ ] Research links are preserved.
- [ ] Analytics and analysis links are preserved.
- [ ] Figma links are attached to the relevant option, iteration, screen, or component.
- [ ] Prototype, screenshot, and review links are not replaced with generic descriptions.
- [ ] Link targets are not fabricated or silently changed.

## Design reasoning

- [ ] Each supplied option has Pros, Cons, and a selection or rejection reason.
- [ ] Meaningful iterations are recorded.
- [ ] Constraints and dependencies are explicit.
- [ ] The chain from Evidence to Decision is understandable.
- [ ] The final outcome may be Proceed, Iterate, Defer, or No change.
- [ ] For Defer or No change, the document states risks, unknowns, and a re-evaluation trigger.

## Writing quality

- [ ] Farsi is clear, natural, and product-oriented.
- [ ] English product terms are used consistently and only where useful.
- [ ] The prose avoids promotion, filler, vague attribution, and unsupported certainty.
- [ ] No information, number, decision, or source link was lost.
- [ ] Headings, tables, and diagrams improve navigation rather than add decoration.

## Humanize pass

- [ ] The register is consistent and appropriate for Product Designers and Product Managers.
- [ ] Inflated significance and promotional language are removed or supported by concrete Evidence.
- [ ] Negative parallelism such as «نه‌تنها ... بلکه ...» is not used as a repeated structural habit.
- [ ] Reflexive triplets, false ranges, filler summaries, and generic conclusions are reduced.
- [ ] Vague attributions and tacked-on analysis are removed or tied to a specific source and claim.
- [ ] Mechanical connectors such as «همچنین»، «علاوه بر این» and «در این راستا» are not repeated without a real logical need.
- [ ] Unnecessary boldface, bullets, headings, emojis, em dashes, and semicolons are removed.
- [ ] Persian `ی` and `ک`, نیم‌فاصله, digits, quotation marks, and punctuation are consistent.
- [ ] No chatbot artifacts, placeholder instructions, or generic closing phrases remain.
- [ ] The pass did not invent facts, personality, sources, numbers, or conclusions.
