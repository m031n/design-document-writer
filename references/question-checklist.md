# Completeness question checklist

Use this checklist during intake. Ask only questions relevant to the material and initiative. Optional sections still require an explicit check.

## Context and Problem

- What is the current system behavior?
- What behavior has actually been observed, and by whom?
- What is the specific User pain, Business impact, Opportunity gap, or Competitive gap?
- What is the measurable or observable impact?
- Which Evidence supports the Problem?
- What is unknown or based on an assumption?

## Goals and scope

- What should users be able to do better?
- What Business outcome should improve?
- What is the expected direction of change?
- What is included in this version?
- What is explicitly excluded?

## Metrics

- Which Primary Metrics matter?
- Which Secondary Metrics or Guardrails protect against harm?
- For each available metric, what are the baseline, target, timeframe, source, and owner?
- Are the denominator, time window, event definition, and population clear?

## Research and Evidence

- Which methods were used?
- Who participated, and how were segments selected?
- What questions did the research answer?
- Where are the raw outputs, analysis, and source links?
- Which findings are direct observations versus interpretation?
- Which benchmark sources were used?
- Are there conflicting findings or evidence gaps?

## Benchmarks and Figma review

- Which competitors, products, internal patterns, or reference experiences were reviewed?
- Why were these examples selected for this Problem?
- Which flows, screens, layouts, components, or interactions were compared?
- Is there a Figma link, and does it point to the relevant file, page, section, or frame?
- Should the linked Figma file be opened and reviewed directly?
- Are there visible Notes, annotations, comments, or comparison tables in Figma?
- Which observations came directly from Figma, and which are the author's interpretation?
- What was discovered through the benchmark?
- Which findings changed a Goal, requirement, Design Option, decision, or Open Question?
- Is a competitor comparison table useful for this initiative?
- If Figma access is unavailable, should the author provide screenshots, exports, or copied Notes?

## Design exploration

- What options were considered?
- What are the Pros and Cons of each option?
- Why was each option rejected or selected?
- Which constraint influenced the decision?
- Which iterations changed the design reasoning?
- Are there Figma, prototype, screenshot, or review links for each stage?

## UX Flow and solution

- Where does the flow start and end?
- What are the main, alternative, loading, empty, success, warning, error, and permission states?
- Which screens and components are involved?
- What are the interaction behaviors and micro-interactions?
- Which edge cases are supported now, deferred, or unsupported?
- Does the proposed solution actually address the stated Problem?

## Decision and follow-up

- What was decided: Proceed, Iterate, Defer, or No change?
- Why was this decision made?
- What trade-offs and risks remain?
- Which Open Questions could change the decision?
- What is Future Work versus an unresolved question?
- What should be checked after launch?
- What signal or trigger should reopen a deferred or unchanged issue?

## Missing information protocol

Ask for missing information once. If the author does not have it, use one of these exact statuses:

- `Not provided`: the author did not include it and has not confirmed it is unavailable.
- `Not available`: the author confirms that it does not exist or cannot be obtained.
- `Not applicable`: the section does not apply to this initiative.
- `Needs validation`: the claim or decision exists but lacks sufficient Evidence.
